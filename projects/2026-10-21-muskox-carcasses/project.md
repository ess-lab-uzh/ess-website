This study investigates the temporal effect of muskox carcasses on the Arctic vegetation greenness in the Zackenberg valley. A Pléiades Neo 3 multispectral satellite image from 2025 and 30 years of muskox carcass locations are used to quantify the carcass effect (difference of NDVI between carcass and paired control plot), and carcasses of different time since death are compared to infer temporal changes (space-for-time substitution). The study also examines how this carcass effect varies among landcover types and topographic conditions (elevation, slope, aspect and topographic wetness index) and between calf and adult carcasses. It also looks at the spatial distribution of the carcasses across the area.
